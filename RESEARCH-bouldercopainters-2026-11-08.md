The 6 sq ft interior / 20 sq ft exterior thresholds were NOT on that page: UNVERIFIED here; cite the regulation text before publishing.
- Boulder historic: city guidance says a Landmark Alteration Certificate is called for when a "significant" color change is proposed; exterior changes in historic districts/landmarks generally need an LAC; interior-only generally not; no fee for the LAC application. Definition of "significant" not found; city suggests calling Planning and Development Services, 303-441-1880. Sources (search results, not individually fetched): https://bouldercolorado.gov/services/landmark-alteration-certificate-application-and-review ; https://bouldercolorado.gov/services/historic-districts ; https://bouldercolorado.gov/landmark-alteration-certificate-lac-review
