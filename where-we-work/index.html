Boulder CO Painters serves Boulder County and the canyon communities above it: Boulder, Lyons, Niwot, Gunbarrel, Longmont, Louisville, Lafayette, Superior, Erie, Nederland and the Peak to Peak, Estes Park, Eldorado Springs and the canyon mouth, Jamestown, Ward and the mountain roads. We do not cover the Denver metro, Golden or the south suburbs. Call 720-208-5645 for a written estimate.

One county, four different climates

Boulder County is unusually varied for its size, and that variation shows up directly in how coatings behave. A fence in Erie and a log wall in Nederland are not the same job with different mileage.

ZoneWhereWhat drives the work
City of BoulderBoulder proper, GunbarrelHistoric districts, altitude UV, foothills wind, student rentals

Where we stop, and why

We do not list Denver, Golden, Broomfield or the south metro. Not because we could not drive there, but because a painting company that claims forty cities is telling you it knows none of them. Boulder County is enough geography for one crew to actually understand.

Not on the list?

Call and ask. If you are just outside the line and the project is right, we will tell you honestly whether it makes sense for both of us.

Questions

Service area questions

What towns does Boulder CO Painters serve?
Boulder and the Boulder County communities around it: Lyons, Niwot, Gunbarrel, Longmont, Louisville, Lafayette, Superior, Erie, Nederland, Eldorado Springs, the mountain communities along the canyon roads, and Estes Park over the hill.
Do you work in Denver or Golden?
No. We cover Boulder County and the canyon communities above it. A company that claims the entire metro usually knows none of it well, and local knowledge is most of what you are paying for.
Do you charge extra to come up a canyon?
Travel is reflected in the written estimate rather than added later. We group canyon projects into single trips where we can, which keeps the number down.
Which areas book up fastest?
Everything, between mid-May and early October. The mountain communities have the shortest season of all, so Nederland and Estes Park projects should be planned for the heart of summer.
