Painting in Lyons, the sandstone town.

Lyons sits where the St. Vrain comes out of the foothills, built from the red sandstone quarried around it. One home in six here predates 1940.

Get a written estimateCall 720-208-5645

Direct answer: We paint homes and small businesses in Lyons (ZIP 80540). It is a town of about 2,200 people and some 760 homes, and 16% of those homes were built in 1939 or earlier (Census ACS 2020–2024), many with wood trim set against local sandstone. Old masonry that has never been painted should generally stay that way. Exterior work follows the same mid-May to September window as Boulder.

Stone, wood and a river

Lyons is known for its red sandstone, quarried in the hills around town and used in buildings across the Front Range. Many older houses here combine that stone with painted wood trim, porches and window frames. The stone is not ours to paint: unpainted masonry breathes, and paint on it tends to trap moisture and fail in a way that is very hard to undo. We protect it while we work on the wood around it.

The town was badly hit by the September 2013 flood on the St. Vrain, and a number of homes were repaired or rebuilt afterward. Those houses are now more than a decade into their finishes.

What we plan for in Lyons

  • Old wood beside stone. Careful masking of sandstone, and scraping and priming of trim and sash.
  • Older homes. 29% of Lyons homes predate 1980 and 16% predate 1940; we check the year for the lead rule.
  • Stain on cedar and timber, common on the newer houses in the hills.
  • Small-town scheduling. We group Lyons jobs on the same days to keep travel out of your price.

Lyons painting questions

Do you paint in Lyons?
Yes, in town and on the properties in the hills around it.
Should I paint the sandstone on my house?
We advise against painting stone that has never been painted. It traps moisture and is very difficult to remove later. We protect it while painting the wood.
How old are Lyons homes?
About 16% of homes in Lyons were built in 1939 or earlier, and 29% before 1980, according to the Census ACS 2020–2024.
When is the painting season in Lyons?
Similar to Boulder: Lyons sits at about 5,340 feet, so mid-May through September is the reliable window.
