Season and altitude

When you can paint outside in Boulder County.

Roughly mid-May to early October at 5,430 feet, and considerably shorter above town. Here is the data and what it means for your project.

Exterior painting in Boulder, Colorado generally runs from mid-May through early October. Denver-area climate normals place the average last spring freeze near May 5 and the average first fall freeze near October 7, and Boulder's elevation of roughly 5,430 feet tightens both ends. The governing number is the overnight low rather than the daytime high, because coatings must cure through the night. Above town toward Nederland and Estes Park, at 8,000 feet and higher, the usable window narrows to the heart of summer.

Overnight lows run the calendar

Homeowners watch the afternoon temperature. Coatings care about the overnight low. A product applied at a pleasant 64 degrees still has to cure through the night, and if the temperature falls below its minimum before the film knits together, adhesion is compromised in ways that do not show for a year.

What elevation does, specifically

  • Ultraviolet intensity rises as you climb. Thinner atmosphere filters less. UV attacks the binder in the coating, producing the fading and chalking you see on south and west walls.
  • Snow reflects it back upward. Lower walls, trim and eave undersides take a second dose all winter, which is why they frequently fail before anything above them.
  • Daily temperature swings are extreme. Forty-degree swings are routine here. Coatings expand and contract through every one of them, and brittle products crack.
  • Chinook winds accelerate downslope. They skin a surface before the film beneath has set, and they carry grit onto it.
  • Humidity is very low. Fast surface dry, slower full cure. The two are not the same thing, and treating them as the same is how lap marks happen.

Reading the forecast like a painter

The questions that actually matter before a crew starts: what is tonight's low, what is the wind doing between noon and four, is there afternoon convection building, and what is the dew point. A beautiful 72-degree day with a 34-degree overnight low and a twenty-mile-an-hour gradient wind is not a painting day, whatever the app says.

Questions

Season questions

What is the best month to paint a house in Boulder?
September, for most exteriors. Stable temperatures, low wind, dry air and fewer afternoon storms than midsummer. June is a close second, and it books out earliest.
Can you paint outside in April?
Occasionally, and we would rather be honest than take a deposit for a bad window. April afternoons can be warm while overnight lows still drop below a coating's minimum cure temperature.
Does Boulder's altitude really shorten paint life?
Yes. Higher ultraviolet intensity at elevation breaks down the binder faster, snow reflects additional UV onto lower surfaces, and extreme daily temperature swings stress the film. All three show up first on south and west elevations.
What about painting in Nederland or Estes Park?
Considerably shorter season. Above 8,000 feet the usable exterior window is effectively the heart of summer, and those projects are scheduled accordingly.
Will you paint on a windy day?
We sequence elevations around it and change application method where we can. Past a certain point we move the day, because grit in wet film is permanent and overspray is somebody else's car.
Can interiors really be painted all winter?
Yes, and it is the smart play. Interior work happens inside a heated building, so the outdoor temperature is irrelevant and the scheduling is far easier.
